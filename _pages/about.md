## Background

I am a final-year Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, concurrently pursuing a Minor in Nanotechnology. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of Fluid Mechanics, Heat & Mass Transfer, and Applied Thermodynamics, with a specific focus on bluff-body wake interactions, turbulence modelling, and the integration of machine learning in combustion modelling.

Driven by a passion for complex thermo-fluid systems, my research spans advanced Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations, including magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) flows and multi-cylinder wake dynamics. I have conducted CFD analysis of a 2 x 2 cylinder arrangement at various angles of orientation and spacing ratios, and explored fin design for heat transfer in micro-channel heat sinks.

Complementing my thermal-fluids background, I undertook a research stint in Solid Mechanics during an internship at the U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), ISRO. There, I worked on the Finite Element Analysis (FEA) of critical ground support equipment used to measure the Centre of Gravity and Moment of Inertia of spacecraft components.

Beyond academics, I am also part of student-led clubs such as Mechismu, the Formula Student racing team of IIT (ISM), and the Black Knight Chess Club.

## Current work

My current research includes the Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) analysis of a 2×2 cylinder configuration. I am working on this in remote collaboration with the Central Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (CSIR-CMERI), targeting applications in offshore engineering, wind engineering, and heat exchanger design. While existing studies have largely focused on the turbulent wake dynamics of highly symmetric configurations, I am addressing this gap by mapping 45 unique cases in the laminar regime across varied spacing ratios (L/D = 1.5, 2.5, and 5), Reynolds numbers (10, 20, and 40), and inclination angles (alpha = 0, 10, 20, 30, and 45 degrees). We aim to publish the complete work by the end of this year and extend the study to slotted cylinder configurations.

Additionally, I am working at the institute's Micro and Nanofluidics Laboratory to develop a novel Manta Ray-inspired fin structure to enhance heat transfer rates in micro-channel heat sinks. Existing literature demonstrates high thermal performance in diamond-shaped profiles; my design modifies this geometry by replacing the leeward side with optimized curved edges to deliberately induce localized turbulence and augment convective heat transfer.
