## Conference Papers

### MHD Flow Around a Heated Stationary Cylinder at Moderate to High Reynolds Numbers
<small>*Presented at [INCOTHERM 2025](https://people.iitism.ac.in/~incotherm2025/); Under Review for Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering, Springer Singapore*</small>

The present study demonstrates a two-dimensional numerical investigation of magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) flow and heat transfer around a stationary circular cylinder subjected to a transverse magnetic field. Simulations are carried out using ANSYS Fluent for an electrically conducting fluid at three Reynolds numbers (Re = 1,000; 10,000; and 100,000) over a range of Hartmann number (Ha = 0-200).


### Rotational Effects on wake Transition in circular rotating cylinder at low Reynolds numbers
<small>*Presented at [INCOTHERM 2025](https://people.iitism.ac.in/~incotherm2025/); Under Review for Lecture Notes in Mechanical Engineering, Springer Singapore*</small>

The present study investigates a two-dimensional, incompressible, laminar, and unsteady fluid flow past a single rotating circular cylinder at various Reynolds numbers (Re = 100, 200, and 300).

## Poster Presentation
### Accelerating Premixed H₂–Air Combustion Simulations Using Neural ODE
<small>*Presented at [SRIP IIT Gandhinagar, 2026](https://srip.iitgn.ac.in/info/)*</small>

This project integrates a Neural Ordinary Differential Equation (Neural-ODE) framework coupled with a Detailed Kinetic Mechanism (DKM) into ANSYS Fluent, using the PyFluent API, to accelerate chemical source-term and heat transfer rate predictions for hydrogen-air combustion, significantly reducing computational overhead while maintaining physical consistency.
